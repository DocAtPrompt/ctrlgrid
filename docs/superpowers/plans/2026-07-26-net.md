# `net` Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** `generator: net` — a tray or a tuck-top box from its inner dimensions, cut lines solid, fold lines dashed, glue tabs computed, refused rather than scaled when it does not fit.

**Architecture:** A style produces **panels** (closed polygons in area-local µm) and nothing else. One rule turns them into marks: an edge shared by two panels is a fold, an edge belonging to one is a cut. Exact, because positions are integer micrometres (§ 3.3). A new style is a panel list, never an outline.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.11+, pydantic v2, integer micrometres, `pytest`, `ruff`.

**Design:** [`2026-07-26-net-design.md`](../specs/2026-07-26-net-design.md) — the § 2 argument, the thickness rule, and what is deliberately out.

### Task 2: the two styles

**Files:** create `ctrlgrid/generators/net_styles.py`, `tests/test_net.py`

- [ ] **Step 1** — failing tests, with `thickness: 0` first because it is the ideal net:
  - **tray**: five panels plus four tabs; the base is exactly `length × width`; the flat size is `length + 2·max(height, glue_tab)` by `width + 2·height`; exactly eight folds (four base edges, four tab edges);
  - **tuck_top**: the wall strip is `2·(length + width) + glue_tab` wide at `thickness: 0`; the lid is `width` deep and the tongue `tuck` deep;
  - **the thickness rule, both directions**: at `thickness: 0.3mm` the lid is 0.3 mm deeper, the tongue 0.3 mm shorter, the tab 0.3 mm shorter, and the wall strip 0.9 mm wider (three wrapped panels).
- [ ] **Step 2** — implement both panel lists. The conventions, stated here and in § 7.14 because they are choices and not derivations:
  - dimensions are **inner**; `t = thickness`;
  - **tray**: base `L × W`; four walls of `H`; a glue tab of `glue_tab − t` at each end of the two end walls, tapered by 2 mm on its free side, spanning the wall's full height so its fold edge matches exactly;
  - **tuck_top**: wall strip `L`, `W + t`, `L + t`, `W + t` (each panel wraps one more layer), glue tab `glue_tab − t` at the end, tapered; on the back panel a lid `W + t` deep carrying a tongue `tuck − t` deep tapered by 3 mm; a dust flap `dust − t` deep on each side panel (`dust: auto` = `min(L/3, 25mm)`); the same closure mirrored below.
  - Every flap spans its attachment edge completely — that is what makes the shared-edge rule exact.
- [ ] **Step 3** — run; **Step 4** — commit: `net: the tray and the tuck-top, from inner dimensions and one thickness rule`.
